7. How we work

How we work

From first call
to signed contract

  1. 01

    We meet

    With the player and the family, at home or at the club. No contract on the first day.

  2. 02

    We plan

    Where the player is now, where he can be in three seasons, and what has to happen first.

  3. 03

    We negotiate

    With clubs, sponsors and lawyers. The player decides; we make sure every option is on the table.

  4. 04

    We stay

    After the signature we follow every match, every injury and every window.
