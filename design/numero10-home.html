What we do

One agent is responsible for each player. Lawyers, financial advisers and communication specialists join when the player needs them.

  • Sports representation

    Transfers, loans and contract renewals, negotiated with clubs. We are present for every conversation about the player's future.

  • Career strategy

    A plan for the next three seasons, reviewed before every window: playing time, league level, coach and style of play.

  • Legal and financial support

    Contract review, tax questions and long-term planning with lawyers and advisers we trust.

  • Image and communication

    Media training, social channels and press requests, handled so the player can focus on football.

  • Commercial partnerships

    Boot deals, brand partnerships and appearances that suit the player and the season.
